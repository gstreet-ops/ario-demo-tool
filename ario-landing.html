How It Works

Three steps. Ongoing intelligence.

A privacy-first data exchange delivering cross-retailer purchase signals you've never had access to before.

01
🎁
Brand makes an offer
You define the value exchange — loyalty points, early access, exclusive perks — presented at the right moment in your app, email, or checkout flow.
Integrates with your existing CRM, loyalty platform, or email stack. No major technical lift required.
02
🔐
Customer connects & consents
Customers opt in through a simple, transparent flow — connecting their accounts at Amazon, Walmart, Target, Wayfair, and dozens of other retailers. Always their choice, always revocable.
Privacy-by-design. Customer-consented. Fully compliant. Ario handles consent management end-to-end.
03
⚡
Brand drives results
SKU-level purchase data flows into your systems — CRM, CDP, personalization engine, ad platform. You see what customers actually buy everywhere, not just with you.
Delivered via API or direct integration. Segment-ready, Klaviyo-compatible, CDP-agnostic.
